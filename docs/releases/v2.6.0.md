# BeReal Studio 2.6.0 — Your memories, mapped

BeReal Studio 2.6.0 gives your archive a new way to be explored. Open **Memories → Map** to see where your posts were made, move from a country into its cities and neighborhoods, and return to the original photos with a click. This release also improves the look of dual-camera exports and adds an Apple Live Photo package that can be brought into Mac Photos.

Everything you import, browse, filter, and export remains on your computer. The map starts with an included world overview that works offline. Detailed street maps are optional and use a key you provide.

## Highlights

- **Rediscover places:** Photo pins, nearby groups, and a gentle density view reveal the places behind your BeReals.
- **Go from photo to place and back:** Open a memory's location on the map, browse every post from that spot, and return to the feed.
- **Take more faithful dual-camera photos with you:** Picture-in-picture exports and previews now sit closer to the look of the original BeReal.
- **Bring a Live Photo into Mac Photos:** A new package option keeps the still photo and its short movie together for a tested Mac import path.
- **Find a moment faster:** Search now recognizes more date and time formats alongside captions and place names.

## Explore your archive on a map

The new Map view is available beside the grid and calendar in Memories. Each memory with saved coordinates can appear as a photo pin. When many posts are close together, they form a group you can open or zoom into. A soft glow across the map gives a quick sense of where the busiest parts of your archive are.

The place list follows the area you are viewing. Start with a country, move into a city, then choose a smaller area to see its memories. Selecting a busy spot opens a gallery, and choosing a photo opens it in the full memory feed. Your usual search and filters apply to the map too, so you can narrow the view to a particular trip, month, or kind of post.

You can also start from a memory. When a photo has a saved location, use its place label in the grid or feed to jump to that exact point on the map. This update fine-tunes the zoom, controls, and pin counts to make those jumps clearer, especially where many memories share one location.

The map includes a world overview and your personal pins without needing a connection. If you want street detail and place labels, open **Memories → Map → Offline map**, enter your own MapTiler key, and choose **Use detailed map**. You can return to the offline view from the same control. MapTiler receives tile requests for the area you view and your IP address; your photos and pin coordinates stay in the app.

Memories without saved coordinates remain in the grid, calendar, and feed. They simply cannot be placed on the map.

## Save photos that look more like your BeReals

The small selfie image in picture-in-picture exports has been adjusted to more closely match the size, position, outline, and rounded shape of a native BeReal. The preview shown in the app has been aligned with the exported result, so it is easier to choose the layout you want before saving. Memory cards and feed controls have also received small navigation and layout refinements.

Search now accepts more familiar ways of typing a date or time. You can still search captions and place names, and the same results carry through to the map.

## Apple Live Photos for Mac Photos

For a photo BeReal that includes a behind-the-scenes clip, the export dialog now offers **Apple Live Photo package (.pvt)**. The package is a folder containing a matching JPEG, MOV, and `metadata.plist`. BeReal Studio gives the photo and movie the same identifier so Mac Photos can recognize them as one Live Photo. A package produced on Windows was checked and imported as one Live Photo in macOS 26 Photos.

To use the tested transfer route:

1. Export **Apple Live Photo package (.pvt)** from a memory, or enable packaged Apple Live Photos in the photo toolkit for a batch.
2. If you are moving it to a Mac, ZIP the `.pvt` folder for transfer and extract it there. The extracted folder must still end in `.pvt`.
3. Open the `.pvt` folder in Finder and confirm that Photos shows one item with the **Live** badge. To move that Live Photo to an iPhone, let iCloud Photos sync the Mac Photos item or AirDrop the item from Mac Photos.

The existing JPEG and MOV pair export remains available. The package is the route tested for Mac Photos import. Sending the raw JPEG and MOV separately to an iPhone will not turn them into one Live Photo. The export needs a behind-the-scenes clip, JPEG output, and FFmpeg; if one is missing, the app now reports the problem instead of leaving an incomplete result. Live wallpaper eligibility and the exact key frame shown by Photos can differ from an iPhone capture.

## Other improvements

- Place links and map controls are easier to find and use with a keyboard.
- Crowded map locations show clearer counts, and a selected memory remains easy to spot among nearby pins.
- Export menus and photo toolkit settings explain when the Live Photo package can be used and how to import it.
- Build instructions now show artifact names for this version, while direct download links continue to point to the latest release that is actually available.

## Get BeReal Studio 2.6.0

When the platform builds have been published, open the [v2.6.0 release page](https://github.com/NotToxel/BeRealStudio/releases/tag/v2.6.0) and choose the package for your computer:

- **Windows:** 64-bit installer or portable app.
- **macOS:** Apple Silicon disk image or application archive.
- **Linux:** AppImage or Debian/Ubuntu package for x86_64.

FFmpeg remains necessary for recap videos, video compositing, and Apple Live Photo MOV creation. You can check for it in **Settings → System & Dependencies**. Your BeReal archive stays on your device throughout import, browsing, and export.
