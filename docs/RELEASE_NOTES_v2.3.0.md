# 🚀 BeReal Studio v2.3.0 — The Ultimate Memories & Video Experience

**BeReal Studio v2.3.0** brings a massive visual, performance, and feature upgrade to your BeReal archive exploration. With full native Video BeReal playback & dual-angle MP4 exports, buttery-smooth 1:1 pointer dragging and zoom, independent 1-click download preferences for every media kind, and a lightning-fast interactive timeline calendar, experiencing and exporting your memories has never been better.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & What's New

### 🎬 1. Complete Video BeReal Support
- **Synchronized Dual-Video Playback**: Experience video BeReals with interactive playback controls, seamless looping, and synchronized audio.
- **Side-by-Side Video Export**: Export both front and back video angles rendered side-by-side into a single high-definition `.mp4` video with original audio sync.
- **Picture-in-Picture Video Export**: Export full-motion BeReals in classic PIP layout directly into `.mp4`.
- **Individual Camera Video Clips**: Save the raw primary video or front selfie video clip independently in a single click.

### 🔍 2. Precision 1:1 Zoom & Pointer Pan
- **High-Definition Exploration**: Zoom in up to 300% on any photo or video memory to view fine details.
- **Fluid 1:1 Pointer Panning**: Click and drag smoothly across zoomed photos and videos without stickiness or missed mouse releases.
- **Dedicated Floating Controls**: Clean, unobtrusive zoom slider and reset pill that appear only when interacting with zoomed media.

### ⚡ 3. Partitioned 1-Click Fast Downloads
- **Media-Specific Smart Defaults**: Your 1-click download settings are now saved and applied independently for **Photos**, **Videos**, and **BTS Micro-Videos**.
- **Zero Conflict**: Saving a default 1-click format for your photos (e.g. High-Res JPEG PIP) will never interfere with your video export preferences (e.g. Side-by-Side MP4).

### 📅 4. Interactive Timeline Scrubber & Calendar Navigation
- **Accurately Aligned Month Markers**: Smooth rail scrubber dynamically mapped across your entire archive timeline.
- **Instant Month & Year Picker**: Jump directly to any month across years in the Calendar view with a single click.
- **Subtle Caption Indicators**: A discreet, ultra-lightweight indicator dot lets you know at a glance which pictures have captions without causing any rendering lag or scroll stutter.

### 🛡️ 5. GPLv3 Copyleft Freedom & 100% Offline Privacy
- **Local-First & Offline**: Your personal photos, videos, locations, and archives never leave your computer.
- **Free & Open Source**: Now officially licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later** to safeguard user software freedom.
