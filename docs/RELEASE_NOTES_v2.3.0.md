## 📋 Comprehensive Changelog & Feature Breakdown

### 🎬 1. Native Video BeReal Experience & Dual-Angle Exports
- **Synchronized Dual-Video Playback**: Native video BeReals play with interactive playback controls, seamless looping, and synchronized dual-camera audio.
- **Side-by-Side Video Export**: Automatically renders both primary and selfie camera video tracks side-by-side into a single high-definition `.mp4` video with perfectly synchronized audio.
- **Picture-in-Picture Video Export**: Export full-motion video BeReals in classic PIP overlay layout with rounded lens borders and drop shadows directly to `.mp4`.
- **Individual Camera Video Extraction**: Save either the primary rear video or front selfie video clip independently in a single click.

### 🔍 2. Precision 1:1 Zoom & Fluid Pan Navigation
- **High-Definition Exploration**: Zoom in up to 300% on any photo or video memory to view subtle background details.
- **Fluid 1:1 Pointer Panning**: Drag smoothly across zoomed photos and videos with instant response and reliable mouse-release tracking.
- **Floating Controls**: Clean, unobtrusive zoom level slider and reset button that appear dynamically only when interacting with zoomed media.

### ⚡ 3. Partitioned 1-Click Fast Download Settings
- **Media-Specific Smart Defaults**: Your 1-click download preferences are now saved and applied independently for **Photos**, **Videos**, and **BTS Micro-Clips**.
- **Zero Configuration Conflict**: Setting a preferred 1-click format for Photos (e.g. High-Res JPEG PIP) will never overwrite your Video export preferences (e.g. Side-by-Side MP4).

### 📅 4. Interactive Timeline & Calendar Navigation
- **Accurately Aligned Month Markers**: Smooth rail scrubber dynamically mapped across your entire archive timeline.
- **Instant Month & Year Picker**: Jump directly to any month across years in the Calendar view with a single click.
- **Subtle Caption Indicators**: A discreet 4px cyan dot indicator lets you know at a glance which pictures have captions without causing any rendering lag or scroll stutter.

### 🛡️ 5. GPLv3 Copyleft Freedom & 100% Local Privacy
- **Local-First & Offline**: Your personal photos, videos, locations, and archives never leave your computer.
- **Free & Open Source**: Now officially licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later** to safeguard user software freedom.
