### [v2.3.0](docs/releases/v2.3.0.md) — 2024-08-26
#### 🌟 Highlights
- **Video BeReal Playback & Dual-Angle Exports**: Side-by-Side & Picture-in-Picture `.mp4` video rendering with synchronized audio.
- **1:1 Fluid Zoom & Pan**: High-DPI 300% zoom with responsive pointer drag and unobtrusive floating controls.
- **Partitioned 1-Click Fast Downloads**: Independent smart defaults and settings storage for Photos, Videos, and BTS micro-clips.
- **Interactive Timeline & Caption Indicators**: Zero-overhead 4px cyan dot caption badges and quick calendar jump.
- **GPLv3 Licensing**: Switched to GNU General Public License v3.0-or-later for user software freedom.

### [v2.0.0](docs/releases/v2.0.0.md) — 2024-08-20
#### 🌟 Highlights
- **Native Memories & Calendar Explorer**: Dark-mode BeReal browsing with multi-column grid and 7-column calendar matrix.
- **Continuous Vertical Infinite-Scroll Feed**: Smooth feed with sticky header and auto-scroll jump.
- **Interactive Dual-Camera Frame**: Inset swapping, draggable PIP, and 4-corner snap hotspots.
- **Rayon Multi-Core Architecture**: Sub-10ms archive parsing and offline spatial reverse geocoding.
